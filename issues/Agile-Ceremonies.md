# Agile Ceremonies Reflection

**How do Agile ceremonies help with communication and alignment?**
Agile ceremonies provide structured, regular opportunities for the team to sync up, discuss potential blockers, and review progress. This continuous feedback loop ensures that everyone is on the same page, preventing siloed work and reducing misunderstandings across different time zones.

**Which ceremony do you think is most important for your role, and why?**
As a Backend Developer Intern, I believe the Daily Stand-up (or asynchronous daily update) is the most important. It allows me to quickly communicate if I am blocked on a specific API endpoint or database integration, ensuring I get help promptly and keeping the backend development aligned with the frontend requirements.

**Document one change you can make to improve your team collaboration:**
I will improve my asynchronous communication by providing highly detailed updates. Whenever I finish a task, I will explicitly link my pull requests, document any API schema changes, and clarify my next steps so that team members in other time zones have full context without needing to wait for a synchronous meeting.
