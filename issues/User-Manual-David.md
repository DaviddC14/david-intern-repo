# User Manual: David Contreras

**Conditions for my best work**
I thrive with clear, detailed instructions and flexibility. Balancing my backend development internship with my 4th-year Systems Engineering studies and family life means I rely heavily on structured, focused blocks of time. Direct communication and well-defined tasks help me execute efficiently.

**Communication preferences**
Asynchronous communication works best for me. Written messages, Slack updates, or well-documented GitHub issues allow me to process technical requirements fully before responding. I am highly responsive during my scheduled work hours.

**Focus and productivity**
I stay productive by utilizing deep work sessions and strictly managing my schedule. Taking short breaks to step away from the screen—sometimes to practice the classical guitar—helps me return to complex backend problems or database designs with a fresh perspective.

**Feedback preferences**
I appreciate direct, constructive feedback, especially during code reviews. Clear, written feedback with examples of how to improve my API structures or database queries is incredibly valuable for my professional growth.

**Quirks and things to know about me**
I often need a few moments to process complex architectural ideas before brainstorming out loud. Outside of work, I love the outdoors—whether it's hiking mountains and volcanoes (like Volcán de Fuego), recreational fishing, or playing soccer.
