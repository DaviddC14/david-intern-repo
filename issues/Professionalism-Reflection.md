# Professionalism at Focus Bear Reflection

**Have you ever experienced or witnessed great teamwork and professionalism? What made it effective?**
Yes, while working on collaborative software projects at university, the most effective teams were those where everyone communicated clearly about their progress and blockers. Respecting each other's code and providing constructive feedback rather than criticism made the development process much smoother.

**What steps can you take to make sure your communication is clear, respectful, and inclusive?**
I will assume good intent in all written messages, avoid sarcasm that can be misinterpreted in text, and be mindful of time zone differences (especially working remotely from El Salvador) when expecting responses from teammates. 

**How can you help create a positive, fun, and professional work environment?**
I can share small wins, use emojis to keep communication friendly, and actively celebrate my teammates' successful pull requests or bug fixes.

**How can you prepare effectively for meetings with supervisors and colleagues?**
I will review the meeting agenda in advance, prepare my specific questions regarding backend logic or database schemas beforehand, and ensure my technical setup (microphone and camera) is ready.

**What would it look like to be proactive during your internship?**
Being proactive means thoroughly reading the documentation before asking for help, independently pulling new tasks from the Kanban board when I finish early, and proposing potential optimizations for our APIs if I spot them.

**How might you follow up with colleagues or supervisors if you need information/action from them? How would you escalate it?**
I would send a polite follow-up message in our original Slack thread after 24 hours. If I am completely blocked and don't receive a response after 48 hours, I would escalate it by tagging my direct manager. If there is a critical server outage or a major security breach, I would escalate immediately.

**What are some things you won't do?**
I will not leave passive-aggressive comments on code reviews, I will not merge code without proper approval, and I will not ignore constructive feedback.
