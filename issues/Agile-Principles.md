# Agile Principles Reflection

**Summarize the main differences between Scrum and Kanban in your own words:**
Scrum is highly structured, working in fixed-length iterations called sprints with specific roles and ceremonies (like sprint planning and reviews). Kanban, on the other hand, is a continuous flow system focused on visualizing work on a board, limiting work-in-progress (WIP), and maximizing efficiency without strict timeboxes. Focus Bear leans towards Kanban to maintain flexibility and ensure continuous delivery without the rigid constraints of sprint deadlines.

**Identify one Agile principle you think will be most useful in your work:**
The principle of "continuous attention to technical excellence and good design enhances agility" is highly relevant to my role as a Backend Developer Intern. It ensures that while we are delivering features efficiently, we are also maintaining a robust and scalable backend architecture. This reduces technical debt and makes managing databases, APIs, and background jobs much smoother in the long run.
