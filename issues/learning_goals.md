# Learning Goals Reflection

**What do you hope to learn from this internship?**
I hope to master enterprise-level backend architecture, specifically working with NestJS, TypeORM, and Docker in a production environment. I want to bridge the gap between my academic projects in systems engineering and real-world, scalable software development.

**What skills are you most excited to develop?**
I am incredibly excited to deepen my knowledge of advanced relational database management with PostgreSQL and robust API design. Additionally, I am looking forward to improving my professional English communication skills while collaborating asynchronously with a remote team.

**What do you think you can contribute to the team?**
I bring a solid foundation in backend development—having built full-stack applications and point-of-sale systems using Node.js, Express, and SQL databases. I also bring a highly proactive, problem-solving attitude and a strong willingness to tackle tedious tasks to help the team move faster.

**What is one area you feel less confident in that you'd like to improve?**
I feel slightly less confident navigating and contributing to a massive, pre-existing enterprise codebase, as most of my previous experience involves building projects from scratch. I want to improve my ability to read, understand, and debug large-scale systems and get more comfortable with complex Git workflows.

**What does success in this internship look like for you?**
Success means successfully shipping my first few backend features or bug fixes to production, seamlessly integrating with the team's daily workflow, and transitioning my mindset from a university student to a capable, reliable junior backend engineer.
