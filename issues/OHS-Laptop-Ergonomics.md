# OHS & Laptop Ergonomics Reflection

**What equipment changes can you make to improve your workspace setup?**
Currently, my setup consists of my laptop, an external keyboard, and an ergonomic chair. While the chair provides good back support and the external keyboard helps my wrists, looking down at the laptop screen causes neck strain. To improve this, I plan to get an external monitor and a laptop stand to elevate my screen to eye level.

**What behavioural changes can you implement to improve posture and reduce strain?**
Even with an ergonomic chair, sitting in the same position for hours while coding is not ideal. I will implement the habit of standing up to stretch regularly to relieve muscle tension and give my eyes a break from the screen.

**How can you remind yourself to maintain good posture and take breaks throughout the day?**
I will use the Focus Bear app to configure automated reminders. When the notification pops up, it will serve as my cue to stand up, stretch my legs and arms, and reset my posture before starting the next block of work.

**Task: Document one workspace change or habit adjustment you made.**
I have officially integrated stretching breaks into my workflow. By stepping away from the desk to stretch, I am actively reducing the physical strain of my backend development tasks. I am also planning the acquisition of the laptop stand to finalize my ergonomic setup.
