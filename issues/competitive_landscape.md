# Competitive Landscape Reflection

**What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?**
Most competitors solve only one half of the problem. Apps like *Freedom* and *Cold Turkey* are excellent at strictly blocking distracting websites and apps, but they lack routine building. On the other hand, *Tiimo* and *Routinery* are fantastic for visual scheduling and guiding users through morning routines, but they don't forcibly block your computer's distractions. Focus Bear is unique because it combines both: it enforces healthy routines (like morning stretching) while simultaneously locking down digital distractions.

**If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?**
I would choose Focus Bear because it is an all-in-one, highly opinionated solution designed specifically with neurodivergent needs (ADHD/Autism) in mind. Instead of juggling a subscription for a website blocker and another for a habit tracker, Focus Bear manages my entire focus lifecycle—from the moment I wake up, through my deep work blocks, to my forced screen breaks.

**What's one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn't?**
*Tiimo* has an incredibly visual, highly aesthetic timeline interface (including excellent Apple Watch integration) that makes viewing your day's schedule feel very intuitive and less rigid. *Cold Turkey* offers a "frozen" lock mode that makes it virtually impossible to bypass the block (even by restarting the computer), which appeals to users needing extreme enforcement.

**Based on your research, what's one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?**
Focus Bear could improve its visual UI/UX to make routine tracking feel less like a strict "takeover" and more like a visually appealing, gamified timeline (similar to Tiimo or Routinery). Additionally, offering more granular, visually rich analytics on focus trends and specific distraction triggers could help users understand their habits better.
